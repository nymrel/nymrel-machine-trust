# Productized Machine Trust audit

Status: **available on request**
Contact: **contact@nymrel.com**

## Buyer outcome

Receive a reproducible evidence packet showing what a public web page declares
to machines, where its machine-readable and rendered surfaces disagree, and
which conclusions the audit cannot support.

## Included

- operator-supplied configuration and public-page input hashes;
- generated JSON-LD, machine indexes, and declared crawler policy;
- a fixed-time scorecard with stable findings;
- rendered-copy parity checks for supplied HTML;
- exact output hashes and generator version;
- a buyer-readable findings and limitations summary.

The fictional [Halcyon Coffee specimen](../specimens/halcyon-coffee/README.md)
shows the complete shape of a deliverable without customer data.

## Evidence boundary

The audit measures configuration, generated artifacts, declared crawler rules,
and supplied rendered HTML. It does **not** measure or promise search rankings,
indexing, crawler compliance, AI citations, recommendations, traffic, leads,
customers, revenue, legal compliance, or future platform behavior.

An A score means the audited inputs satisfied this package's current checks.
It is not an endorsement by a search engine, AI provider, standards body, or
customer.

## Intake

The safe precursor is a public URL plus the desired audit outcome. Do not send
credentials, private customer records, secrets, production access, or payment
data. Scope, schedule, and any commercial terms remain operator-reviewed and
on request.

## Protected gates

This repository does not execute outreach, accept payment, publish customer
results, access provider consoles, or mutate production. Those actions require
separate authority and evidence.
